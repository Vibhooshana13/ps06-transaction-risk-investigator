How It Works

PS06 — Banking / Fraud Desk

This assistant reviews a single customer's transaction history against a set of deterministic risk rules. It never claims fraud — it only highlights activity that deviates from that customer's own baseline behavior and marks the account for human review.

The Rules

  • Large Transfers — flags transactions well above the customer's typical transaction size.
  • New-Payee Bursts — flags a cluster of payments to payees the customer has never paid before, sent in a short window.
  • Odd-Hours Activity — flags transactions made at hours the customer doesn't normally transact in.
  • Pattern Deviation — flags overall behavior that breaks from the customer's established pattern (frequency, amount, or destination).

What the Verdict Means

Flagged means one or more rules triggered and the account needs human review — it is not a fraud determination. Routine means the transaction history is consistent with the customer's own baseline.

Why No Fraud Detection Model?

The rules are fully deterministic and run with zero LLM calls, so every flag can be traced back to the exact transactions that caused it. The AI is only used afterward, to turn the rule engine's findings into a readable narrative report — it never makes the flagging decision itself.
